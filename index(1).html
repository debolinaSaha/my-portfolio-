GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering undergraduate at Meghnad Saha Institute of Technology (MSIT), Kolkata, graduating in July 2027, with an academic SGPA average of 8.51. My research interests span Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Image Processing, IoT and Embedded Systems. I have contributed to multiple research publications in Springer-indexed and Scopus-indexed proceedings, covering applications such as lunar image enhancement, examination misconduct detection, intelligent surveillance and agricultural disease detection. I previously interned in AI/ML and Video Analytics at the Centre for Railway Information Systems (CRIS), Indian Railways (September 2025 – March 2026). My achievements include Second Runner-Up at the 9th ICCPDM 2026, a filed patent application (IN202631079536), and a Government of India software copyright registration for ChaturDrishti. My technical interests include Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, OpenCV, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi, with a growing focus on Data Engineering and Edge AI. I aim to contribute to research-driven engineering and develop practical, intelligent technology solutions.
